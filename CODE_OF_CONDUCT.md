# Code of conduct

Project Graphite is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Be considerate, give actionable feedback, respect differing experience levels, and keep technical
disagreements focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate disruption
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. To report conduct privately, contact an organization owner using the contact information on
their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
